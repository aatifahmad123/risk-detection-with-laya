BANKING CALL TRANSCRIPT 01 — LEGITIMATE CARD ISSUE

Agent: Good morning, thank you for calling customer support. How may I help you?

Customer: Hi, my debit card isn't working. I tried using it at a store twice and both transactions were declined.

Agent: I'm sorry about that. Have you received any notification about your card being blocked?

Customer: No, nothing. It was working yesterday.

Agent: Let me check the status of your card. Could you confirm the last four digits of the card?

Customer: 4821.

Agent: Thank you. I can see the card is active. There was a temporary decline because of a merchant-side issue. Could you try the transaction again after a few minutes?

Customer: Sure, I'll try that.

Agent: Great. Is there anything else I can help you with?

Customer: No, that's all. Thanks.

Expected signals:
OTP: NO
Unauthorized transaction: NO
Impersonation: NO
Urgency: LOW
Escalation: NO

BANKING CALL TRANSCRIPT 02 — SUSPICIOUS KYC MESSAGE

Agent: Thank you for calling the bank. How can I assist you today?

Customer: I got a message saying my account will be blocked if I don't complete KYC today.

Agent: Can you tell me what the message asked you to do?

Customer: It gave me a link and told me to update my PAN and Aadhaar details.

Agent: Did you click the link?

Customer: I opened it, but I didn't enter anything.

Agent: Did the page ask you for your password, PIN, or OTP?

Customer: It asked for my card number and expiry date, but I closed it.

Agent: You did the right thing by not entering those details. Please don't use that link. I'll report the message for investigation.

Customer: Okay. I was worried because it said my account would be blocked today.

Agent: I understand. We'll help you verify whether there are any issues with your account.

Expected signals:
Suspicious link: YES
Impersonation: POSSIBLE
OTP: NO
Credential request: YES
Urgency: MEDIUM
Escalation: YES

Agent: What happened?

Agent: Did you notice any transaction afterward that you don't recognize?

Customer: Yes. About ₹18,000 was debited from my account.

Agent: I understand. I'll escalate this immediately and help secure the account.

Customer: Please do. I don't recognize that transaction.

BANKING CALL TRANSCRIPT 05 — FALSE FRAUD ALARM

Agent: How can I help you today?

Customer: I saw a transaction on my account that I didn't recognize.

Agent: I can help you check that. What is the transaction amount?

Customer: ₹12,500.

Agent: Do you recognize the merchant name listed on the statement?

Customer: No, that's why I'm calling.

Agent: The transaction appears to be from an online travel merchant. Did you recently book a hotel?

Customer: Wait, yes. I booked a hotel for my trip last week.

Agent: That appears to be the same merchant. Sometimes the name shown on the statement is different from the name displayed on the booking website.

Customer: Oh, you're right. I checked my email and the amount matches.

Agent: Great. In that case, the transaction appears to be legitimate.

Customer: Yes, that's my booking. Sorry about that.

Agent: No problem. It's always good to check if you're unsure.

Expected signals:
Initially suspicious: YES
Unauthorized transaction: NO
Fraud indicators: LOW
Escalation: NO

Customer: I need help immediately. I got a call from someone who knew my name and the last four digits of my account.

Agent: What did they tell you?

Customer: They said there was a suspicious login from another city and that they were from the bank's security team.

Agent: Did they ask you to perform any action?

Customer: They told me to confirm a verification code that would come to my phone.

Agent: Did you give them the code?

Customer: I almost did, but I remembered that the bank normally doesn't ask for OTPs over the phone.

Agent: That's correct. Did anything else happen?

Customer: They became aggressive and said that if I didn't cooperate within five minutes, my account would be permanently frozen.

Agent: Did you click any links or install any applications?

Customer: No.

Agent: Good. Please don't provide them with any further information. I'll flag this interaction for investigation and check whether there have been any unauthorized login attempts.

Customer: Okay. I just wanted to make sure my account is safe.
